# Before publishing a superlative about VistA, check these two

Searched 2026-08-14 while wording the landing page's opening claim. Both
counter-examples are real, current, and the first thing an informed reader reaches for.

**"First … to AI" — SSA got there first, in name.** SSA announced a **three-year, $1B
AI-assisted modernization** of its 60M+ line COBOL codebase in **April 2025**. That is AI
pointed at a nationwide federal system before this work. The honest distinction is *what
kind* of AI relationship: SSA is a **one-time AI-assisted migration** (COBOL→Java, the
same translation that failed in 2017, with no public evidence it has shipped); vista-forge
makes the system **continuously AI-operable** — agents develop, manage, and modernize it
on gates that prove each change. Claim the second thing, never the bare "first".

**"Largest" — depends entirely on the metric.** VHA is documented as *the largest
**integrated** health care system in the US*: **1,380 facilities**, 170 medical centers,
1,193 outpatient sites, **9.1M enrolled**. But **DoD's MHS GENESIS** covers **9.6M
beneficiaries** across every military hospital and clinic worldwide (full deployment
March 2024) — *more people*. So "largest" survives only with the **integrated** /
sites-of-care qualifier, and dies on head count.

Also in the neighbourhood, if the comparison ever comes up: **IHS RPMS** is a VistA
derivative across 36 states (family, not competition); the IRS Individual Master File and
SSA are all-50-state federal systems too, so "nationwide federal information system" is
not itself distinguishing — VistA's is that it **delivers care** rather than moving money.

## The rule this produced

Prefer **checkable scale facts** over rankings. What shipped: *"VistA — the VA's integrated
healthcare information system, serving over 1,200 hospitals and clinics nationwide for over
40 years — has been comprehensively re-engineered to provide AI-integrated development and
modernization."* Every clause is verifiable — 1,380 facilities is the published figure, so
"over 1,200" is deliberately conservative — and none of it invites the SSA or DoD rebuttal.

⚠️ **Watch the attribution, not just the superlative.** The draft said "VA's VistA **is**
the first … to leapfrog," which reads as *the VA* having done this. vista-forge is an
independent effort; the shipped sentence describes VistA as the VA's system and states the
re-engineering without crediting it to them. A claim can be true and still misattribute.

Standard caveat, per [[negative-claims-from-bounded-searches]] in the shared store: four
searches is not proof no other counter-example exists.
